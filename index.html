開枱！台灣牌係個人項目。使用本 App 即表示你接受本政策。

我哋會存咩

  • 手機入面會存一個隨機 userId，用嚟坐位同段位。呢個唔係 Google 或者 Apple 帳戶。
  • 你可以自己改顯示名稱、頭像同背景。開枱之後其他玩家會見到。
  • 網上對戰會把出牌、食糊、枱號傳到我哋伺服器，等四個人可以一齊打。
  • 單機成績留喺你部手機。
  • 我哋可能留短連接紀錄，方便修 bug。

我哋唔會做嘅事

  • 唔賣你嘅資料，唔出廣告。
  • 唔讀通訊錄、相簿、位置。
  • 唔要求你登入 Google / Apple。
  • 籌碼只係局內計分，唔涉及真錢，亦唔可以兌換現金。

刪除資料

刪除 App 就會清走手機入面嘅資料。如果要我哋刪伺服器紀錄，電郵 admin@cashmate.hk，主旨寫「刪除資料」，同埋你嘅顯示名稱或者 userId。

本 App 唔係專為 13 歲以下兒童而設。

查詢：admin@cashmate.hk

English

Kaitai Taiwan Mahjong (開枱！台灣牌) is a personal project. Using the app means you accept this policy.

We store a random userId on your phone for seats and rank. This is not a Google or Apple account. You may set a display name, avatar and background. These show at the table. Online play sends discards, wins and table codes to our server so four people can play. Solo stats stay on your phone. We may keep short connection logs to fix bugs.

We do not sell data, run ads, read contacts, photos or location, or require login. Chips are in-game score only. No real money, no cash-out.

Delete the app to clear on-device data. Email admin@cashmate.hk with subject 刪除資料 to ask us to drop server records for your userId.

Not directed at children under 13. Contact: admin@cashmate.hk
